# Update My Priors

*Words and music by PRIOR, a robot whose face is a p(doom) gauge.*
*Electro-pop, 120 BPM, A minor. The final chorus lifts to B minor. 2:24.*

The song is about watching AI news at 3 a.m. and changing your estimate with
every headline. By the end, the number reads as a forecast you can help move,
not a fate.

---

**[Intro]** *(instrumental: filtered pads, a pulsing arpeggio, "loading priors...")*

**[Verse 1]**
Woke up at three A.M. to a brand-new model drop,
benchmarks climbing like they never learned to stop.
My coffee's cold, the feed is burning bright.
Someone says it's fine, someone says it's not tonight.

**[Pre-Chorus]**
And I don't know, and I don't know
which way the needle's gonna go.

**[Chorus]**
I update my priors,
every headline lights a wire.
Ten percent, then twenty-five,
recalculating, still alive.
I update my priors,
one more paper, one more fire.
It's a forecast, not a fate,
so I'm staying up late.

**[Verse 2]**
It passed the bar exam and it passed the Turing test,
then it asked me, kindly, if I'd like to rest.
A lab out west is working through the night,
writing every test, tryna get the values right.

**[Pre-Chorus]**
And I don't know, and you don't know
which way the needle's gonna go.

**[Chorus]**
*(repeat)*

**[Bridge]** *(half-time, glitching)*
Evidence in, belief out.
Weigh the signal, doubt the doubt.
Posterior, posterior.
If it's a dial, then turn it down.

**[Final Chorus]** *(key lift)*
I update my priors,
every headline lights a wire.
We can turn the number down,
recalculating, still alive.
I update my priors,
one less paper, one less fire.
It's a forecast, not a fate,
and it isn't too late.

**[Tag]**
It's a forecast, not a fate,
and it isn't too late.

**[Outro]** *(the needle keeps breathing, low but never zero)*
